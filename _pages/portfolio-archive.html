This portfolio combines institutional systems, internal tools, research software, and public-facing web experiences. My work spans backend logic, integrations, frontend delivery, and practical problem solving for both operational and editorial teams.

Featured Projects

1. Digital Diploma Integration

Situation: Ifes needed to align its graduation workflows with Brazil's official Digital Diploma platform. Task: Help implement a reliable integration for a high-stakes academic process. Action: I contributed to the evolution and integration of institutional systems connected to diploma issuance. Result: The solution enabled the issuance of more than 5,000 valid digital diplomas by March 2025.

2. Student Aid Management System

Situation: Campus Montanha needed a better way to manage student meal assistance. Task: Build a system that could support daily operations with less manual work. Action: I designed and implemented a dedicated platform for the process. Result: The system supports the management of around 1,000 subsidized meals per day.

3. MIT Technology Review Interactive Projects

Situation: Editorial and sponsored content teams needed polished, production-ready interactive experiences. Task: Deliver custom pages with data-driven visuals and responsive front-end behavior. Action: I contributed to multiple projects through Infobase, using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, D3, and Chart.js. Result: The work shipped on public-facing pages for MIT Technology Review and other recognized brands.
