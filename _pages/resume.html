Profile

Backend / Full-Stack Engineer with 10+ years of experience building and maintaining business-critical systems, APIs, and internal platforms. My background combines backend development, systems integration, legacy modernization, and hands-on support for production environments.

I have delivered software for public institutions, research environments, and recognized digital brands, with most of my recent work centered on Java, Spring Boot, PHP, JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, and web platforms. I am based in Brazil and open to remote international software engineering roles.

  • Designed and implemented a student aid management system supporting around 1,000 subsidized meals per day
  • Delivered projects for organizations including MIT Technology Review, Fiocruz, Deloitte, FGV, and Globo Filmes

Core Technologies

  • Workflow and delivery: Git, GitLab, CI/CD, Agile delivery, production support
