SICOPE was developed at the NINFA laboratory at UFES as part of a research project focused on optimizing investments to reduce non-technical electrical losses, in partnership with EDP Escelsa and EDP Bandeirante.

Between 2012 and 2013, I contributed to the project's graphical user interface, using Python and wxPython.
